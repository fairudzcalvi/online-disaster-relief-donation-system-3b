About BayanihanRelief

A student-led initiative bringing transparency and efficiency to disaster relief in the Philippines

Our Story

BayanihanRelief was born from a simple observation: in times of disaster, generous Filipinos want to help, but often struggle to find transparent, trustworthy channels for their donations.

As students, we witnessed how natural calamities affect our fellow Filipinos, and we saw an opportunity to bridge the gap between willing donors and verified relief organizations. We created this platform to make disaster relief donations more accessible, transparent, and impactful.

Our name comes from "Bayanihan," the Filipino tradition of community unity and cooperation. We believe that by working together and ensuring transparency in every donation, we can rebuild hope for families affected by disasters.

Student-Led Initiative

Our Mission

To provide a transparent, secure platform that connects compassionate donors with verified relief organizations, ensuring every peso reaches families in need during disasters and crises.

Our Vision

To become the most trusted student-led disaster relief platform in the Philippines, where transparency and community support transform how we respond to crises together.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Transparency

We believe donors have the right to know exactly where their contributions go and how they're used.

Trust

We partner only with verified organizations and maintain the highest standards of accountability.

Compassion

Every decision we make is guided by empathy for the communities we serve.

Community

We believe in the power of Bayanihan – working together to lift each other up.
